Personalised
Matrix factorisation · SVD

Surprise's biased SVD (Funk-style, the Netflix Prize recipe). Trained on user–restaurant ratings.

Learns latent vectors for every user and every restaurant; predicts a 1–5 star rating, normalised to [0,1].

r̂(u, i) = μ + b_u + b_i + q_iᵀ · p_u

Cold start: if user is unknown, returns 0.0 for every candidate.

Content-Based
TF-IDF · Rocchio profile

Each restaurant → TF-IDF vector over categories, price level, and attributes (takeout, outdoor, …).

Returning: Rocchio profile = rating-weighted average of TF-IDF vectors for liked places, centred on 3.0.

New: onboarding cuisines, attributes & price → tokens → vectorised.

score = cos(p_user, v_item)

Popularity
Bayesian average · IMDB-style

Shrinks each restaurant's mean rating toward the global mean — prevents a single 5-star review from winning.

v m s = ——— · R + ——— · C v+m v+m

R mean rating · v reviews · m median (prior) · C global mean. Non-personalised.

Distance
Exponential decay

Closer is better, smoothly. No hard cliff — just a soft decay from the user's location.

score = exp(−d_km / 5.0)

1.0 at user · ≈0.37 at 5 km · ≈0.14 at 10 km.
